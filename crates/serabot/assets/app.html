Invitation-only · privately hosted

Your own agents, kept private.

SeraBot is a small, invitation-only agent workspace. Every member gets their own bots, memory, documents and connected accounts, isolated from everyone else, with approvals before anything destructive happens.

What it does

A small circle, a capable assistant

Bots that are yours

Create bots with their own role, picture and model, and group them into teams that work together on a task.

Memory and documents

Bots remember what you tell them and answer from your own documents with citations. Nothing is shared between members.

Your accounts, your connection

Connect OneDrive, Outlook, Calendar, Notion or Google yourself. Each credential is encrypted per member and never visible to the operator.

Approval where it matters

Deleting memory, sending email or uploading files always asks you first. Everything is recorded in an audit trail.

Wherever you are

Use the Windows desktop console, the Android app, or chat with your bots on Telegram.

Untrusted by default

Web pages, files and model output are treated as data, never as instructions, and outgoing actions pass through one policy gateway.

Access

By invitation

SeraBot has no public sign-up. You need an invitation from the operator, then you sign in with your Microsoft account. Downloading a client does not grant access, and no credential is embedded in any build.
